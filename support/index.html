Support

Need help with Tagline Inspect?

  • Cannot sign in or forgot your password: ask your company administrator to invite you again or reset your password.
  • Lost phone: ask the administrator to wipe and revoke the device.
  • Sync does not finish: check your internet connection and tap "Sync now". Work you did offline stays on the phone until it is sent.
  • Anything else: write to contact@coralcell.com with your name, company and a short description (add a screenshot if you can).
