Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Tagline Inspect is built and operated by Coralcell Limited (United Kingdom) for TÜV Rheinland Arabia. Your employer (TÜV Rheinland Arabia) decides who gets an account and is the controller of the inspection data; Coralcell Limited processes it on its behalf.

What the app stores

  • Account: your name, work email, role and permissions, created by your company administrator.
  • Inspection work: job orders, equipment details, checklist results, inspection reports, hours and daily work reports you enter.
  • Photos and signatures: photos you take of equipment (camera) or pick from your library, and the receiver signature drawn on screen.
  • Stickers and stock: the sticker and report-book numbers assigned to you.
  • Device information: device name and model, system version and app version, so the company can list and, if a phone is lost, wipe it or block it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no sale of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts or microphone.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used to photograph equipment and scan sticker QR codes. The photo library is used only for photos you choose to add. Face ID or Touch ID, if you turn it on, only unlocks the app on your phone; your biometric data never leaves the device and is never seen by us.

Where data goes and how it is protected

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the company server and stored there. On the phone, the sign-in session is kept in the iOS keychain and the local data is protected by an app PIN. A company administrator can end a session or wipe a lost phone remotely.

How long we keep data

Inspection records and certificates are kept as long as TÜV Rheinland Arabia needs them for its work and legal duties. When an account is closed, its access ends.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete your personal data, contact your company administrator or write to contact@coralcell.com. To delete the data on the phone, sign out or remove the app.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not for children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
