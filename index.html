Tagline Inspect

Tagline Inspect is the field app of the Tagline inspection and certification platform, built by Coralcell Limited for TÜV Rheinland Arabia. Inspectors use it to see their job orders, inspect equipment, fill checklists, take photos, collect the receiver signature, use stickers from their stock, log hours and send everything to the office. It keeps working without a network and syncs when the phone is online.

The app is for staff who have an account from their company administrator. There is no public sign-up.
